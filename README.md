# Credit-Risk-Collections-Analytics | Power BI
## Project Overview

Credit Risk & Collections Analytics is an **end-to-end Business Intelligence case study** developed using Microsoft Power BI, focused on financial portfolio analysis, credit risk assessment, and debt collection performance.

Starting from provided Excel datasets and business requirements, the project covers the complete analytical process, including data cleaning and transformation, data modeling, DAX calculations, KPI development, interactive dashboard design, and actionable business insights.

The project consists of two complementary dashboards designed to evaluate financial exposure, monitor collection activities, identify portfolio risks, and support data-driven decision-making.

## Business Objectives

The analysis focuses on five key business areas:

- **Credit Risk Assessment:** Evaluate outstanding balances, missed payments, and risk exposure across loan portfolios.
- **Portfolio Segmentation:** Analyze financial exposure by loan type, customer age group, collection status, and geographical region.
- **Collections Performance:** Monitor recovered amounts, collection targets, achievement rates, and performance gaps.
- **Agent Productivity:** Compare collection results across agents to identify performance differences and opportunities for improvement.
- **Recovery Optimization:** Identify portfolio segments requiring greater collection attention and recommend operational improvements.

## End-to-End Workflow

The project was developed through the following stages:

### 1. Data Understanding

Review of the provided Excel datasets and business requirements to identify relevant financial indicators, analytical dimensions, and reporting objectives.

### 2. Data Preparation & Transformation

Data cleaning and transformation using Power Query to prepare the datasets for analysis and reporting.

### 3. Data Modeling

Organization of financial portfolio and collection information into a data model supporting analysis across loan types, collection statuses, risk segments, regions, and agents.

### 4. DAX & KPI Development

Development of analytical calculations and performance metrics, including:

- Total Loan Amount
- Total Outstanding Balance
- Average Missed Payments
- Total Amount Collected
- Collection Targets
- Achievement Percentage
- Gap to Target

### 5. Dashboard Development

Design and development of two interactive Power BI dashboards using KPI cards, comparison charts, analytical tables, trend visualizations, and dynamic slicers.

### 6. Business Analysis & Insights

Interpretation of analytical results to identify risk exposure, performance gaps, recovery opportunities, and operational recommendations.
